Academic Policies Handbook

Course Registration
Students must register for courses through the Student Portal during their assigned registration window, which is based on class standing (seniors register first, followed by juniors, sophomores, and freshmen). The add/drop period lasts for the first two weeks of each semester. Courses dropped after this period but before the withdrawal deadline will appear on the transcript with a "W" grade.

Grading System
The university uses a 4.0 grading scale. A letter grade of A corresponds to 4.0 grade points, A- to 3.7, B+ to 3.3, B to 3.0, B- to 2.7, C+ to 2.3, C to 2.0, C- to 1.7, D to 1.0, and F to 0.0. A cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 is required to remain in good academic standing.

Academic Probation
Students whose cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 for two consecutive semesters are placed on academic probation. Students on probation must meet with an academic advisor and may be limited to a reduced course load. Failure to raise the GPA above 2.0 within two semesters of probation may result in academic dismissal.

Attendance Policy
Regular class attendance is expected. Individual instructors set their own attendance policies within their syllabi, but students who miss more than 20% of scheduled class sessions without an approved excuse may be administratively withdrawn from the course.

Academic Integrity
Plagiarism, cheating, and other forms of academic dishonesty are serious violations of university policy. A first offense typically results in a failing grade on the assignment or course, while repeated offenses can lead to suspension or expulsion. All suspected violations are reviewed by the Academic Integrity Board.

Graduation Requirements
To graduate with a bachelor's degree, students must complete a minimum of 120 credit hours, satisfy all general education requirements, complete all requirements for their declared major with a GPA of 2.0 or higher in major coursework, and file a graduation application with the Registrar's Office by the deadline in their final semester.
