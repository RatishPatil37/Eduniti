Admissions FAQ

What is the application deadline for the Fall semester?
The application deadline for Fall semester admission is March 15. Applications submitted after this date will be considered for the Spring semester only if space is available.

What documents are required for undergraduate admission?
Applicants must submit a completed application form, official high school transcripts, two letters of recommendation, a personal statement, and standardized test scores (SAT or ACT), if applicable. International applicants must also submit proof of English proficiency such as TOEFL or IELTS scores.

Is there an application fee?
Yes, the non-refundable application fee is $60 for domestic applicants and $75 for international applicants. Fee waivers are available for students who demonstrate financial need; contact the Admissions Office to request one.

What is the minimum GPA required for admission?
There is no strict minimum GPA, but competitive applicants typically have a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher on a 4.0 scale. Admission decisions consider the whole application, not GPA alone.

Can I transfer credits from another institution?
Yes. Transfer credit is evaluated on a course-by-course basis. Students must submit official transcripts from all previously attended institutions. Generally, only courses with a grade of C or higher are eligible for transfer, and a maximum of 60 credits may be transferred toward a bachelor's degree.

How do I check my application status?
Applicants can check their status at any time through the online Applicant Portal using the login credentials provided after submission. Status updates typically occur within 5 business days of receiving new materials.

When will I receive my admission decision?
Admission decisions for Fall semester applicants are typically released by early April. Early Action applicants who apply by November 1 receive decisions by mid-December.
